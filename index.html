Athleta is a social sports app that helps people find pickup games, see which nearby sports fields are active, and play with friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the control you have over it.

Athleta is operated by Pragnay ("we", "us"). If you have questions or want your data deleted, contact pragnaywork@gmail.com.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Location data, including background location

Athleta asks for background location access. Here is exactly what that means.

What background location is used for

Only to detect when you enter or leave a sports zone. Your phone's operating system watches the boundaries of your saved zones and tells Athleta when you cross one. Athleta then records an "entered" or "left" event for that zone. Background location is not used for anything else.

What we do not do

  • We do not continuously log your position or keep a trail of where you go. What is stored is that you entered or left a specific sports zone, and when.
  • We do not sell location data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show other users your entry/exit history. Live player counts are totals per zone and do not identify who is present.

Location sharing settings

  • Who can see it: Friends Only (the default) or Everyone. Users under 18 are always Friends Only (see section 4).
  • How long: a live-location share lasts for the time you choose: 5, 10, 20, 30 or 60 minutes.
  • Turn it off at any time in the app's settings, or revoke location permission in your phone's settings. Revoking background permission stops automatic zone detection; the rest of the app keeps working.

What other users can see

  • Everyone: verified sports zones, your display name and your avatar.
  • Friends, or everyone, depending on your setting: your live location and current activity status.
  • Members of a community you join: your name in the roster, your RSVPs, your attendance, and your messages in that community's chat.
  • Nobody: your email, phone number, date of birth, password, zone entry/exit history and how you voted on fields.

4. Children and teens

Athleta is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. For users who tell us they are under 18:

  • Location visibility is forced to Friends Only and cannot be changed to Everyone.
  • Adults cannot start a direct message with a minor unless they are already mutual friends.

Age is self-reported and is not verified with identity documents. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact pragnaywork@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, preferences, goals, points, RSVPs, attendance and push tokens: until you delete your account.
  • Zone entry/exit events: until you delete your account. Only the last three hours are used, for live player counts.
  • Community chat messages: until you delete your account.
  • Sports zones: unverified zones are deleted with your account. Verified zones stay on the map without your name, because other players use them.
  • Communities and events you created: stay available to their other members, without your name.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in either of two ways:

  • In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • By email: if you can no longer use the app, email pragnaywork@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Athleta account". We will confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, preferences, goals, points, RSVPs, attendance records, community memberships, community chat messages, zone entry/exit events, field confirmation votes, push tokens and unverified zones. What we keep is described in section 5. Data stored only on your device, such as direct messages and your profile photo, is cleared from the app when you delete your account in the app, and is removed if you uninstall it.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • Stop sharing your location, or turn off notifications.
  • Leave any community without needing approval.
  • Delete your account (section 6).
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for something to be corrected, by emailing pragnaywork@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, PIPEDA, or applicable provincial or state privacy laws. We handle requests the same way wherever you are: email the address below.

8. Security

Data is stored on Supabase and protected by row-level security rules, so each user can only read the data they are entitled to. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and its "last updated" date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
