Athleta is a social sports app that helps people find pickup games, see which nearby sports fields are active, and play with friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the control you have over it.

Athleta is operated by Pragnay ("we", "us"). If you have questions or want your data deleted, contact pragnaywork@gmail.com.

2. Location data — the important part

Athleta requests background location access. We want to be specific about what that means.

What we use it for

  • Detecting when you arrive at or leave a sports zone. Your device watches the boundaries of sports zones and records an "entered" or "left" event. This is what powers live player counts, activity heatmaps, automatic goal tracking and attendance.
  • Sharing your live location with people you choose, according to your privacy settings.
  • Showing you nearby zones and how far away they are.

What we do not do

  • We do not continuously log or store a history of everywhere you go. What is stored is a timestamped record that you entered or left a specific sports zone — not a location trail.
  • We do not sell location data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not let other users read your raw entry/exit history. Live player counts are aggregated totals per zone and do not identify who is present.

Your controls

  • Visibility: choose whether your live location is visible to Friends Only or Everyone. Friends Only is the default.
  • Duration: choose how long a live-location share lasts (5, 10, 20, 30 or 60 minutes).
  • Stop any time: turn location sharing off in Settings, or revoke the permission in your device settings. Revoking background permission disables automatic activity detection but the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Children and young people

Athleta is not intended for children under 13. For users who tell us they are under 18:

  • Location visibility is forced to Friends Only and cannot be set to Everyone.
  • Adults cannot start a direct message with a minor unless the two are already mutual friends. Within a community, an adult organiser can only message a minor in the community's group chat.

Age is self-reported; we do not verify it with identity documents. If you believe a child has given us data without appropriate consent, contact pragnaywork@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

What other users can see

  • Public: sports zones you create once verified, and your display name and avatar.
  • Friends, or everyone, depending on your setting: your live location and current activity status.
  • Community members: your name in the roster, your RSVPs, your attendance, and messages you send in that community's chat.
  • Nobody: your email, phone number, birthdate and password.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Zone entry/exit events: retained until you delete your account. Only the last three hours are ever read, for live player counts; older rows are not surfaced anywhere in the app.
  • Messages: until deleted by you or the community owner.
  • Sports zones: zones you create may remain visible to the community after you leave, since other people rely on them.

6. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • Stop sharing your location.
  • Block or report another user, or report a sports zone.
  • Leave any community without needing approval.
  • Request a copy of your data, or request deletion, by emailing pragnaywork@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, PIPEDA, or applicable provincial or state privacy legislation. Requests are handled the same way regardless of where you are: email the address below.

7. Security

Data is stored on Supabase and protected by row-level security rules so that each user can only read the data they are entitled to. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and change the "last updated" date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
